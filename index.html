The problems we're working on

Each challenge is tied to a measurable target and an open working group anyone can join.

Water

Save the lakes, fix the pipes

Bangalore's 200+ lakes are its kidneys. We're mapping encroachments, tracking sewage inflows, and piloting decentralised treatment at ward level to reclaim 30 lakes by 2028.

Mobility

Untangle the 72-minute commute

Open-sourcing BMTC bus data, building last-mile feeder route models for Namma Metro, and crowdsourcing signal-timing fixes at the 50 worst junctions in the city.

Waste

Close the dry-waste gap

Only 40% of Bangalore's dry waste is segregated. We're working with BBMP and waste-picker cooperatives on a ward-by-ward collection dashboard and informal-sector integration plan.

Governance

Make ward committees work

198 wards, barely a dozen active committees. We publish attendance data, budget utilisation scores, and citizen-complaint resolution rates to drive accountability.

Flooding

Map the flood corridors

Combining ISRO elevation data with stormwater drain surveys to build a live flood-risk model. Goal: early warnings to every affected ward 6 hours before a cloudbreak.

Air Quality

Breathe cleaner by 2030

Deploying a low-cost sensor mesh across 50 high-traffic corridors to build a real-time AQI map and give KSPCB the evidence for targeted interventions.

How we work

Open data, open code, open meetings. Every working group follows the same rhythm.

Measure it first

No intervention without a baseline. We scrape, FOIA, and sensor-deploy until the problem has a number attached.

Build the smallest useful tool

A dashboard, a bot, a data feed. Ship a working prototype to the people closest to the problem within 8 weeks.

Partner with the system

BBMP, BWSSB, BMTC, KSPCB, ward committees. Handoff is the goal; shadow infrastructure isn't.

Show progress publicly

Monthly scorecards. Every dataset on GitHub. Every meeting recorded. If we can't show the change, we haven't made it.
